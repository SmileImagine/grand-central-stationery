## Why the English words need special scrutiny

The Philippine National Commission for Culture and the Arts essay ["Defining the Filipino Through Song"](https://ncca.gov.ph/about-culture-and-arts/in-focus/defining-the-filipino-through-song/) warns that schoolbook practice of translating Filipino songs into English and teaching only the translation can obscure their cultural origin. This record therefore labels the printed words a historical English classroom adaptation. It does not call them traditional Ilocano lyrics.
