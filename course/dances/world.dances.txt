// Basic steps of social and folk dances from beyond the ballroom, typed in the
// lab's step language as they are taught in schools and community classes.
// They are traditional steps, nobody's choreography, and several are simplified
// (said so in the note). z is a step across behind. Where hips, shoulders or
// hands carry the dance, the note says so; the language only has feet and arms.
// Ceremonial and sacred dances are left out on purpose. This file is CC0.
source: traditional steps, typed for Grand Central Stationery
licence: CC0

// ---- Latin America and the Caribbean ----

title: Salsa basic (on 1)
origin: Cuba, Puerto Rico, New York
meter: 4/4
bpm: 95
note: break forward on 1, back on 5; the fourth and eighth counts are held.
feet: Lf Rp Lp . | Rb Lp Rp .
arms: o  o  o  . | o  o  o  .

title: Merengue basic
origin: Dominican Republic
meter: 4/4
bpm: 130
note: a march with the hips: the weight-free hip lifts on every step.
feet: Ls Rc Ls Rc
arms: h  h  h  h

title: Bachata basic
origin: Dominican Republic
meter: 4/4
bpm: 120
note: three steps to the side and a tap with a hip lift on four, then back.
feet: Ls Rc Ls rc | Rs Lc Rs lc
arms: h  h  h  h  | h  h  h  h

title: Samba basic (no bounce)
origin: Brazil
meter: 2/4
bpm: 100
note: step, close, step forward then back; the knees give the bounce that the language cannot write.
feet: Lf-Rc Lp | Rb-Lc Rp
arms: o     o  | o     o

title: Cumbia basic
origin: Colombia
meter: 4/4
bpm: 95
note: the back step and replace, then forward with a tap.
feet: Lb Rp Lf rc | Rb Lp Rf lc
arms: o  o  o  o  | o  o  o  o

title: Zapateado threes
origin: Mexico (jarabe, son jarocho)
meter: 6/8
bpm: 110
note: three stamps and a rest, alternating feet; the stamps are the rhythm.
feet: Lp Rp Lp . . . | Rp Lp Rp . . .
arms: d  d  d  . . . | d  d  d  . . .

// ---- Africa and the diaspora ----

title: Kpanlogo basic
origin: Ga (Ghana)
meter: 4/4
bpm: 110
note: simplified: step side and touch, with the arms pushing out and in at the chest.
feet: Ls rc Rs lc
arms: o  c  o  c

title: Hambone step
origin: African-American
meter: 4/4
bpm: 100
note: the feet walk while the hands pat the thighs and slap the chest: see the Hambone rhythm.
feet: Lp Rp Lp Rp
arms: h  c  h  c

title: East Coast swing basic
origin: African-American (Lindy hop, jitterbug)
meter: 2/4
bpm: 90
note: triple step, triple step, rock step: six counts.
feet: Ls-Rc Ls | Rs-Lc Rs | Lb Rp
arms: o     o  | o     o  | o  o

title: Hip-hop two-step
origin: African-American
meter: 4/4
bpm: 95
note: step side and together to each side, with a bounce in the knees.
feet: Ls Rc Rs Lc
arms: d  d  d  d

title: Gumboot stamp and slap
origin: South Africa (isicathulo)
meter: 4/4
bpm: 100
note: stamp, then slap the boot: the arms line's claps stand for the slaps.
feet: Lp . Rp . | Lp Rp Lp .
arms: d  c d  c | d  d  d  c

// ---- the Middle East, the Balkans and Greece ----

title: Dabke basic
origin: the Levant
meter: 4/4
bpm: 115
note: simplified: step, cross, step, stamp with the heel; the line holds hands.
feet: Ls Rx Ls rp
arms: d  d  d  d

title: Hora (Hava Nagila)
origin: Israel, Romania
meter: 2/4
bpm: 130
note: six counts: side, behind, side, hop with a kick, side, hop with a kick; the circle moves to the left.
feet: Ls Rz | Ls H | Rs H
arms: o  o  | o  o | o  o

title: Mayim step
origin: Israel
meter: 4/4
bpm: 120
note: the grapevine the dance is built on: across, side, behind, side.
feet: Rx Ls Rz Ls
arms: o  o  o  o

title: Pravo horo
origin: Bulgaria
meter: 2/4
bpm: 110
note: simplified: two steps forward, step and lift, step back and lift; the line moves right.
feet: Rf Lf | Rs lc | Lb rc
arms: d  d  | d  d  | d  d

title: Hasapiko basic
origin: Greece
meter: 4/4
bpm: 90
note: simplified: step side, cross behind, step side, kick across; then the other way.
feet: Rs Lz Rs K | Ls Rz Ls K
arms: o  o  o  o | o  o  o  o

title: Kalamatianos step
origin: Greece
meter: 7/8
bpm: 150
note: the twelve-step dance reduced to one bar of long, short, short: step right, step left, step right, lift.
feet: Rs Lx Rs
arms: o  o  o

// ---- South and East Asia, the Pacific ----

title: Bhangra bounce
origin: Punjab
meter: 4/4
bpm: 110
note: a bounce on each foot with the arms up and the shoulders lifting; simplified.
feet: Lp-H Rp-H Lp-H Rp-H
arms: u    u    u    u

title: Garba three-step
origin: Gujarat
meter: 6/8
bpm: 120
note: simplified: three steps and a clap, moving round the circle.
feet: Rf Lf Rf . . . | Lf Rf Lf . . .
arms: o  o  o  c . . | o  o  o  c . .

title: Hula kaholo
origin: Hawai'i
meter: 4/4
bpm: 100
note: four steps to the right and four to the left, the hips following; the arms extend to the side you travel.
feet: Rs Lc Rs lc | Ls Rc Ls rc
arms: o  o  o  o  | o  o  o  o

title: Hula hela
origin: Hawai'i
meter: 4/4
bpm: 100
note: point the foot forward and bring it back, each side; the weight stays over the standing foot.
feet: rf Rc lf Lc
arms: o  o  o  o

title: Tinikling basic
origin: Philippines
meter: 3/4
bpm: 110
note: the step between the bamboo poles: out on one, in, in; then out the other side. Simplified to steps on the floor.
feet: Ls Rs Ls | Rs Ls Rs
arms: o  o  o  | o  o  o

// ---- Ireland and Scotland ----

title: Sevens and threes
origin: Ireland (céilí)
meter: 4/4
bpm: 110
note: seven side steps to the right over four beats, then two threes in place.
feet: Rs-Lc Rs-Lc Rs-Lc Rs | Lp Rp Lp . | Rp Lp Rp .
arms: d     d     d     d  | d  d  d  . | d  d  d  .

title: Pas de basque
origin: Scotland (country dance)
meter: 2/4
bpm: 110
note: spring onto one foot, step the other across in front on the ball, step in place; then the other side.
feet: Ls Rx-Lp | Rs Lx-Rp
arms: o  o     | o  o
