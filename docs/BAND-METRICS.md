## Thinner sample sets

A set may keep only every few notes and retune the nearest for the rest (`SAMPLE_STEP` in index.html). The cost: the log-spectral distance between each note from G3 to C5 and its neighbour retuned to it (dB, third-octave bands 100 Hz to 8 kHz, median). Every third note kept means a semitone at most and a third of the download; every fourth, a tone and a quarter.

## Reading the columns

- **flat 9th**: share of the melody's time with a band note a semitone under it (in any octave). **above**: with a chord note at or above it. **fit**: the melody's strong-beat notes that are in the chord.
- **mud**: chords (with the bass under them) breaking a low interval limit. **top move**: semitones the chords' top voice moves at a change. **roots**: downbeat bass notes on the root.
- **drums / bass / chords ms**: the spread of onsets around the grid, the part's own lean taken out. A sequencer is 0; a good player is a few ms.
- **accent**: correlation of the bass's velocity with the beat's strength. **answered**: the band's onsets a beat in the melody's holes (two beats or more) against its average; above 1 it plays more where the melody leaves room. **drum repeat**: how alike each bar's drums are to the last (1 the same). **fill variety**: the last two beats before every fourth bar line, every time round, that differ from one another (distinct fills per fill; hats and cymbals aside). **fill repeat**: fills the same as the one before. **fill drums**: the drums a fill moves between. **snare vel spread**: the snare's strength, its spread against its average. **arc**: the fullest time round's weight against the lightest.
- **LUFS**: integrated loudness (BS.1770). **melody LU**: the melody alone against the band alone. **masked**: share of the melody's three strongest third-octave bands (frame by frame) where the band is as loud. **tilt**: slope of the octave-band spectrum, 63 Hz to 8 kHz. **band 200–500**: the band alone, its share of energy in the low mids. **width**: side against mid (0 is mono). **cpu ×**: seconds to render a second of the band offline. **samples KB**: the sample files the play loads.
