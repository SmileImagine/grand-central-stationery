Drag a box over the lesson's colour cue (the lit bell, the coloured note). Its colour is matched to the Boomwhacker colours Prodigies uses (C red, D orange, E yellow, F green, G teal, A purple, B pink) and the bell lights with no listening at all.

What is possible, and what is not

Prodigies' lessons stream from play.prodigies.com (Uscreen) behind a login, in a player of another origin, so no page can read their sound or picture through the player itself. Everything here works from the outside: the microphone, the browser's tab sharing, or a file.

Melody is a monophonic pitch track (McLeod's normalised square difference with a stability gate). Bells and a singing voice track well; a piano accompaniment under the melody confuses it, so use All notes for those passages, which lights every pitch class with energy but cannot say the octave.

Look-ahead (the on-deck key and the runner) needs the future, so it exists only for a file, which is analysed in full before it plays. Live sources show the note as it sounds, about 50 ms late.

Colour reads the shared picture, so it does not care what is playing. It needs the cue to be a solid patch of one colour; a box over a coloured bell, note head or hand works, a box over the whole frame does not.
