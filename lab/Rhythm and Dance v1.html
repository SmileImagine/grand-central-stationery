Rhythm & Dance lab v1

One line per drum. name: steps, the steps of a bar sharing it equally: x hit, X accent, o soft, . rest; spaces are ignored, | starts a new bar. A line with 3 steps against one with 2 is a polyrhythm. meter: 3/4 sets the beats in a bar. Drums: kick snare hat open rim clap tom low wood bell clave shaker; body: stomp clap pat snap click. The Body view plays every line as body percussion. The Syllables view speaks each beat in Kodály words, fruit words (pear, ap-ple, wa-ter-mel-on), counting (1 e & a, onsets only), takadimi, or Carnatic konnakol (ta ka di mi for four, ta ki ta for three, ta di gi na tom for five). The stick notation above the words shows onsets, not durations.

Seen from turns the dancer round: from behind, left is on the left; from in front, the figure is mirrored, so a class copying the teacher can follow it. The Columns keep the dancer's own left and right, as Labanotation does.

One token per beat. feet: a foot and a direction: L/R step onto it, l/r touch without weight; f forward b back s side x across z across behind c close (Ls step left to the side). J jump, H hop, K kick, . hold, Ls-Rc two moves in one beat. arms: u up o out d down c clap h hips . hold. The Rhythm tab's pattern plays underneath.
