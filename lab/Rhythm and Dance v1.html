One line per drum. name: steps, the steps of a bar sharing it equally: x hit, X accent, o soft, . rest; spaces are ignored, | starts a new bar. A line with 3 steps against one with 2 is a polyrhythm. meter: 3/4 sets the beats in a bar. Drums: kick snare hat open rim clap tom low wood bell clave shaker; body: stomp clap pat snap click. The Body view plays every line as body percussion. The Syllables view speaks each beat in Kodály words, fruit words (beat, cher-ry, pine-ap-ple, av-o-ca-do, blue-ber-ry, mel-on and wa-ter-mel-on for notes held over beats), counting (1 e & a, onsets only), takadimi, or Carnatic konnakol (ta ka di mi for four, ta ki ta for three, ta di gi na tom for five). The stick notation above the words shows onsets, not durations.

A round: round: 4 every=1 has four groups play the pattern, each coming in a bar after the one before (every=2b is two beats). Each group has a numbered marker in every view and its number where it comes in; with Repeat on they go round until Repeat is turned off, then stop one by one (end=together stops them all with group 1). The faders under the text set each group's level as it plays, and a click on a group's number mutes it, so the class can take that group.

Poses shape the whole body, the way DanceWriting draws it: pose reach: arms=165,175 hand=spread stretch=1.1 defines one, and poses: reach . . . calls it on a count (. holds, 0 lets go). Limbs are two angles each (upper and lower) in degrees from hanging down, outward positive, with f or b when a segment comes forward or goes behind (armR=90ff,90ff points straight ahead); legL=/legR= lift a gesture leg. Also lean side twist stretch hips shoulders look nod tilt, hand=flat fist spread point cup, foot=flat point flex ball. Trace a pose… makes the line from a photo or a video frame.

Writing draws the dancer the way Sutton DanceWriting does, on five lines for the floor, knees, hips, shoulders and head, so rising and sinking read off the lines. An open circle at a hand or foot is forward of the body, a filled one behind. The drums and the steps scroll past behind the figure in notation; whatever passes under the dancer is now.

Seen from turns the dancer round: from behind, left is on the left; from in front, the figure is mirrored, so a class copying the teacher can follow it. The Columns keep the dancer's own left and right, as Labanotation does.

One token per beat. feet: a foot and a direction: L/R step onto it, l/r touch without weight; f forward b back s side x across z across behind c close (Ls step left to the side). J jump, H hop, K kick, . hold, Ls-Rc two moves in one beat. Effort marks after a token shape how it moves: ! sudden ~ sustained _ strong ^ light @ indirect (curving) = bound (no anticipation). Shape lines: level: l m h sinking and rising, lean: f b l r c for the shoulders, hips: l r f b c pushes or tilts the pelvis, shoulders: l r u c lifts a shoulder or both, turn: r or l per quarter turn (rr a half), or degrees (r45 an eighth, to a diagonal), after which forward is the new way. arms: u up o out d down c clap h hips . hold. The Rhythm tab's pattern plays underneath.

Mark a segment f when it comes toward the camera, b when it goes away; how far comes from how short it looks. With no picture, drag the figure into a pose by hand.
