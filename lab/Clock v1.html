The grand clock

The piece view's one play and tempo control (course/clock.js, course/clock.css), floating over the top-left of the score where abcjs draws its tempo mark. Click or tap the face to play; drag the face or the tempo up or down to change the tempo in fives (hold Shift for ones); arrow keys and the wheel do the same; double-click the tempo for full speed. The hand sweeps once a bar and lights each beat it passes. The loop round the face (|: and :| with arrows over and under) is the repeat toggle; paused partway, a small round button at the end of the plate goes back to the start.
